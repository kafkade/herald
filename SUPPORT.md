## Getting Help

1. **Check existing issues** — your question may already be answered:
   [github.com/kafkade/herald/issues](https://github.com/kafkade/herald/issues)

2. **Open a new issue** — use the appropriate template:
   [github.com/kafkade/herald/issues/new/choose](https://github.com/kafkade/herald/issues/new/choose)

3. **Discussions** (when enabled) — for questions, ideas, and general conversation:
   [github.com/kafkade/herald/discussions](https://github.com/kafkade/herald/discussions)

## Security Issues

Please see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) for reporting vulnerabilities. Do not
open public issues for security concerns.
